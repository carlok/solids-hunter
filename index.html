Solids Hunter is a first-person hunt: each round you get a boolean rule (sometimes simple, sometimes with AND, OR, or NOT). Only raycast-shoot solids that satisfy the rule.

  • Move: WASD / arrow keys, gamepad left stick, or mobile left thumb pad
  • Look: mouse (pointer lock after you start), gamepad right stick, or drag the right side of the screen on mobile
  • Shoot: left-click, gamepad RT/R2, or mobile FIRE button
  • Gamepad: A starts/resumes; Start/Menu pauses
  • Rule: shown in full on the hunt screen; during play it stays at the top center of the HUD
  • Sound: M toggles mute anytime; arena / rule / pause screens also have a Sound: ON/OFF button; in-game, top-left icon or M (mute also silences spoken coach lines)
  • Hit coach: arena / rule / pause — cycle Coach: OFF / VOICE / MODAL. Wrong hits: voice-only pauses targets briefly with a subtitle; modal+voice unlocks the cursor until you confirm. Correct hits stay instant; coach adds optional spoken tips. Speech picks the best available English voice from your browser (see MDN) and reads a short line aloud (like Agility Trainer); the modal still shows the full text. Set localStorage.solidsHunterHitConfirmN to limit coaching to the first N hits per round (0 = unlimited).
  • Pause: Esc — opens pause; click Resume to continue
  • Menu: ☰ top-left — Help, Credits, Main menu

Solids move in different ways (drift, bounce, orbit, slide). Correct match +10 points. Wrong target −5. Clear every matching solid to finish the round.

Rule check

OK unlocks when the coach finishes speaking. Then click the arena to capture the mouse again.
