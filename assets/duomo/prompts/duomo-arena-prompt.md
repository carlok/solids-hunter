# Solidshunter Duomo Arena Prompt

Create a fifth Solidshunter arena called `duomo`, inspired by the internal plan and spatial rhythm of Milan Cathedral.

The result should not be an architectural reconstruction. It should be a playable arena that captures the experience of a forest of tall Gothic pillars:
- repeated vertical polystyle shafts,
- five-aisle nave rhythm,
- wider transept interruption,
- narrower choir and apse suggestion,
- engaged wall piers and perimeter massing,
- pale marble floor and softly fogged daylight.

Use the saved rhythm map at `assets/duomo/maps/milan-duomo-plan-rhythm.json` as the structural source.

Keep gameplay clear:
- targets must remain visible,
- the spawn area must be open,
- collisions should be simple and predictable,
- decorative architectural details should be non-blocking unless explicitly used as perimeter walls or major pillars.

The ceiling is intentionally omitted for now. The goal is an open-air cathedral skeleton: columns, aisles, rhythm, and scale.
